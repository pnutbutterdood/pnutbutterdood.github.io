🛑 Air Brakes Numbers & Pressures

  • Governor Cut-out: 120 - 140 psi
  • Governor Cut-in: ~100 psi
  • Low Air Pressure Warning: Below 60 psi
  • Spring Brakes Pop Out (Automatic): 20 - 45 psi
  • Air Leak Rate (Single Vehicle): < 2 psi / min
  • Air Leak Rate (Combination): < 3 psi / min

🛞 Treads, Distances & Inspections

  • Front Steering Tire Tread: At least 4/32 inch
  • Other Tires Tread Depth: At least 2/32 inch
  • Cargo Check Frequency: First 50 miles, then 150 mi / 3 hrs
  • Reflective Triangles Placement: 10 ft, 100 ft, 200 ft
  • Air Brake Lag Distance at 55 MPH: Adds ~32 feet
