Quick Study Cheatsheet

Essential air pressure figures, leak rates, confirmed undivided highway triangles, fire extinguisher classes, and CDL suspension rules from the official Michigan CDL Manual.

🛑 Air Brakes Pressures & Leak Tests

  • Governor Cut-out: 120 - 140 psi
  • Governor Cut-in: ~100 psi
  • Low Air Pressure Warning: Below 60 psi
  • Spring Brakes Pop Out (Automatic): 20 - 45 psi
  • Single Vehicle Leak Rate (Engine Off, Brakes Applied): < 3 psi / min
  • Combination Leak Rate (Engine Off, Brakes Applied): < 4 psi / min
  • Static Air Loss Rate (Brakes Released): < 2 psi/min (single) / < 3 psi/min (combo)

⚠️ Triangles & Fire Extinguishers

  • Divided / One-Way Highway Triangles: 10 ft, 100 ft, 200 ft (back)
  • Undivided / Two-Way Highway Triangles: 10 ft (front/rear), 100 ft (back & ahead)
  • Placarded Vehicle Fire Extinguisher: UL Rating 10 B:C or more
  • Class A Fires (Wood, Paper): Extinguish by Cooling/Water
  • Class B Fires (Gas, Oil, Grease): Smothering/CO2/Dry Chemical
  • Class C Fires (Electrical): Non-conducting agents (NEVER water)

⚖️ CDL Major & Serious Violations & Suspensions

Major Offenses (DUI, Felony, Hit & Run)

1st Conviction: 1-year suspension (3 years if transporting Hazmat).

2nd Conviction: Minimum 10-year revocation.

Controlled Substance Felony / Human Trafficking: Lifetime revocation.

Serious Traffic Violations (Speeding, Tailgating)

2 Convictions within 3 years: 60 days suspension.

3+ Convictions within 3 years: 120 days suspension.

Railroad Crossing (1st / 2nd / 3rd): 60 days / 120 days / 1 year suspension.
